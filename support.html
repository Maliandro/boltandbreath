Frequently asked questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
The game itself plays offline and you don't need an account. A connection is only needed to load ads and to verify purchases.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
Yes, both. The game is free to download; ads are shown through Google AdMob and there are optional in-app purchases. You can decline the iOS tracking permission — ads just won't be personalised, and nothing in the game changes.

Where is my progress stored?
Only on your device. If you delete the app, your save is deleted too.

Privacy

Privacy policy — in short: your progress stays on your device; our advertising partner processes limited data.
