Privacy Policy

Bolt & Breath · Effective date: 10 September 2026

Summary: Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, which means a limited set of data is shared with Google. In-app purchases are processed by Apple; your payment details never reach us.

1. Data controller

The data controller for this app is the independent developer who publishes it, Mehmet Ali Dengiş. For any request or question about your personal data, contact: mealidengis@gmail.com. The seller information on the App Store belongs to the same developer.

2. Legal basis for processing

Personalised advertising relies solely on your explicit consent: the iOS tracking permission and (if you are in the European Economic Area) the advertising consent form. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised and the game works exactly the same.

The limited technical data processed for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention relies on the legitimate interest of offering the app free of charge.

Purchases rely on the performance of a contract and are handled entirely by Apple.

3. What we collect as the developer

Nothing. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and you do not need to create an account. The only information that reaches us directly is whatever you choose to share when you write to us by email.

4. Advertising and data processed by our advertising partner

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To select, serve and measure ads, AdMob may process data such as:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS — only if you allow it),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level),
  • device model, operating system version, language and similar technical information,
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, time watched).

We cannot see this data; we only receive anonymous, aggregated performance reports. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

iOS asks for your separate permission before your advertising identifier can be used. You can choose not to allow it. In that case ads are not personalised; no game feature is switched off, no level is locked and performance does not change. You can withdraw permission later in iOS › Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Your card number, name and billing information stay with Apple and are not passed on to us. The only thing we see is anonymous, aggregated sales statistics from App Store Connect. Refund requests are handled through Apple.

7. What is stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, Ember Hearts balance, upgrades, sound and display settings) is kept only in the app's own storage on your device. This data does not leave your device. Deleting the app also deletes these records.

To detect technical problems, the app may keep a local error log on your device. This file stays on your device and is not sent to anyone unless you explicitly share it.

Notifications: the app schedules notifications such as the daily reward reminder on your device itself (local notifications). No data is sent to us or any third party for these notifications, and no push notification server is used. You can turn off notification permission in iOS Settings, and the reminders in the game's Settings menu, at any time.

8. Third-party services

Third-party services used in the app: Google AdMob (advertising) and Apple (distribution, purchases, anonymous crash reports). There are no social media integrations, sign-in systems or separate analytics SDKs.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe your child's data has been processed, write to us at the address below and we will take the necessary steps to have the relevant records deleted.

10. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection to processing and data portability.

As the developer, we hold no records about you — so there is no record to delete if you send us an erasure request. However, our advertising partner Google processes the data listed in section 4 in its own systems; you can send requests about that data through Google's privacy tools. You can also withdraw your tracking permission at any time as described in section 5.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye, or with the data protection authority of your country in the European Economic Area.

11. International data transfers

Google and Apple, which provide the advertising and distribution services, are companies headquartered outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. The advertising data listed in section 4 and purchase information may be processed on these companies' servers abroad. Such transfers take place under the standard contractual clauses and privacy policies these companies have committed to.

12. Retention

Because we do not collect data from you as the developer, we hold no records about you. The game save on your device stays there until you delete the app. Retention periods for data processed by our advertising partner are set out in Google's own privacy policy.

13. Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page and the effective date will be changed. If the scope of data processing changes, the update will be clearly announced together with the app release.

14. Contact

mealidengis@gmail.com
